# I/O 0.2.0 — Community and discovery

- Reply to episode comments, like/unlike comments and mark spoilers. Spoilers stay hidden until opened.
- Browse paginated discussions and load more replies. Deleting a comment preserves its replies.
- Report a comment with a reason. Administrators can open **Discussion reports** from their profile menu, dismiss reports or remove comments, and filter completed reviews. Duplicate reports are prevented and review actions are recorded.
- Discover real genre-based collections and use **Show me different stuff** to refresh collection choices and featured titles. Profile content filtering applies before titles enter the collections.
- Choose Light, Indigo or Black on the login screen. The selection is saved on that browser.

The discussion database upgrade is automatic and preserves existing comments. No external credentials or new services are needed for these features. Live TV, external sign-in/email recovery and remaining screen-level parity are still outstanding.
